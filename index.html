    Questions

    Is the payment real?

    No. This is a demo. The payment windows are simulated, no money moves and card details are never stored or sent anywhere.

    How long is my seat held?

    Fifteen minutes past your start time. After that it goes back on the floor.

    Can I bring friends?

    Yes. Book neighbouring seats, or use a console pit for up to four players. Teams of five or more should ask about the Bootcamp Room.

    Can I order food to my seat?

    Yes. Open the Order food tab, pick your seat and add it to your bill or pay now.

    Where is my booking saved?

    In this browser only. Clear your site data and it disappears.
